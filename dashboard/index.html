snapshot model
How the implied value is built. log10(market cap) = intercept + slope x log10(TVL) + category intercept + growth, age and chain terms The slope and intercept are fitted by OLS on the 128 live-token protocols of this same snapshot; a category's intercept is its mean log-residual, shrunk by n / (n + 5). The 90% range is conformal, from 5-fold held-out errors. Launch odds are the Laplace-smoothed share of protocols of the same age and size that already trade a token.
Every figure comes from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot written beside this page; nothing is fetched, and no protocol is listed or ranked by name. An implied value is a model's claim, not a promise, and not make.fun's.
